\section{Conclusion}
The complete feature representation with the Neural Network provides the strongest observed predictive performance, with F1 0.4738 and balanced accuracy 0.6765. Feature selection reduces dimensionality by 70.59\% and improves some classifiers, especially K-NN under univariate and wrapper selection and Decision Tree under the filters, but does not outperform the strongest full-feature model.

Reduced representations make the retained employee characteristics easier to interpret. Business travel, job involvement, job role, marital status, monthly income, overtime and stock-option level are selected by all three strategies at original-variable level. They identify recurring classification-related associations in this dataset, without supporting causal conclusions. The research question is therefore answered by a trade-off: consistent characteristics emerge from selection, while the complete representation retains the strongest predictive performance.
